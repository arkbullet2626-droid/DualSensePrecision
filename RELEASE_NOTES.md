**Dual Precision 1.0.0** by Opifex Studios: free controller tuning for Windows.

**[Download DualPrecision-Setup.exe](https://github.com/arkbullet2626-droid/DualSensePrecision/releases/latest/download/DualPrecision-Setup.exe)** (about 66 MB). Windows 10 (1809+) or 11, 64-bit. Installs just for your Windows account: no administrator rights needed for the app.

## What it does

- **Sticks your way:** inner and outer deadzones, anti-deadzone, sensitivity, circle or square output and response curves you can drag, with presets like Competitive FPS, Precision Aiming and Racing.
- **Triggers your way:** deadzones, curves, hair-trigger mode and, on DualSense, adaptive trigger effects.
- **Remap anything:** any button to another button, a keyboard key or a mouse button, a shift layer, and macros.
- **Works in games:** a virtual Xbox controller (via the free VIIPER add-on) carries your settings into almost any PC game. **Hide your real controller** (via the free HidHide add-on) so games see one controller, not two.
- **Measured, not guessed:** live report-rate and latency from the controller itself, and a Controller Tester for drift, range, triggers and every button.
- **Profiles** with templates for Fortnite, Call of Duty, Rocket League, Rust, ARC Raiders and racing games.
- **Make it yours:** 12 colour themes (including light ones) or your own colours, interface scaling, and DualSense lightbar, player lights and vibration.
- Runs in the tray, can start with Windows, and checks GitHub for updates (can be switched off).

Supports the **PlayStation 5 DualSense** (USB) and **Xbox controllers** (Series X|S, One, Elite, 360; USB, Bluetooth or Xbox Wireless Adapter).

## Installing

1. Run `DualPrecision-Setup.exe`. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info → Run anyway**.
2. Tick **VIIPER** (virtual controller) and **HidHide** (hide your real controller) if you want them. Each asks Windows for permission because it installs a signed driver; HidHide's own installer opens and needs a few clicks.
3. Restart Windows if asked, then turn on the virtual controller on the **Virtual Controller** page.

Updating from an earlier version installs over it and keeps your profiles and settings. Setup closes a running copy for you.

**Note:** some games ban macros or remapping tools in online play. Check a game's rules before using them there.

## Privacy

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Settings and profiles stay on your PC (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\DualSensePrecision`). The only thing the app does online is an optional check of GitHub for a newer version number. Full policy: https://opifexstudios.ca/#dual-precision-privacy
